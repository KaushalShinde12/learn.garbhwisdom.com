What the program includes

There are 3 pillars of Garbh Wisdom Holistic Yoga Program:

01

Garbh Samskar led Dincharya

Includes the following-

  • Garbh Samwad - A pdf is shared on tips of Fetal Talk for the new moms
  • Garbh Aahar - Trimester specific Pregnancy Diet is shared (Separate charges)
  • Garbh Naad - Group Mantra chanting sessions are conducted weekly
  • Garbhini Paricharya - A pdf is shared on Prenatal Self Care Tips
  • Garbh Vichar - Scientific tools such as affirmations and visualization are practiced to train the subconscious mind and break the old patterns.
  • Garbh Vyavhar - Dincharya is discussed as per your current lifestyle, tips shared
  • Garbh Circle - Exclusive access to join a supportive community of expecting mothers to share experiences, ask questions, and prioritize your mental well-being.

02

Garbh Yogabhyasa

Includes the following-

  • Trimester Specific Prenatal Hatha Yoga Asanas
  • Strengthening Asanas - To maintain muscles mass during pregnancy
  • Posture Correction Asanas - Release backpain, pelvic pain, ribcage pain
  • Chair Supported Asanas - For mobility and opening of pelvic and shoulder joints
  • Pelvic Openers - To prepare body for labor
  • Pranayama Practise (pregnancy safe breathing practices)
  • Garbh Dhyana (Meditation upon garbh)
  • Aum Chanting
  • Mudras & Kriyas

03

Labour & Childbirth Education

Includes the following-

  • Labour Drills post 32nd Week
  • Labour Breathwork - LAMAZ Breathing
  • Labour Simulations - Prepare pelvic floor memory for the real labour
  • Theory Segment - 5 mins important topic covered in every class
  • Birth Plan Design Guidance
  • Pooja Nair will be available on call personally to monitor the labour.

🌸 Process to Join the Class 🌸

First of all, big congratulations to you, ji! 🎉 We're so excited to support you during this beautiful journey.

Before we begin, here are a few steps to ensure your safety and readiness for the classes:

1) Share Your Latest Scan Report

This helps us understand your pregnancy stage and tailor the sessions accordingly.

2) Approval from Your Gynae

Please provide a heads-up or verbal approval from your gynecologist confirming you're cleared for prenatal yoga and related activities.

3) Medical History

Let us know if this is your first pregnancy or if there's any medical history (e.g., previous conditions or complications) that we should be aware of for your safety and well-being.

💖 Once these details are shared, we'll guide you on the next steps to begin your Garbh Sanskar-inspired journey with us!
